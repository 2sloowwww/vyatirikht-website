How Often Should a Small Business Post? The Honest Answer

Social Media & Business · 4 August 2026 · 4 min read

The gurus say daily. Your schedule says never. The truth sits somewhere specific — and it has more to do with consistency than volume.

The Question Behind the Question

When a business owner asks how often to post, they are really asking: what is the minimum effective dose? They have a shop to run. The guru answer — post daily, post everywhere, ride every trend — is written by people whose entire business is posting. For everyone else, it produces three weeks of enthusiasm followed by a dead account, and a dead account signals worse than a modest one.

What the Algorithm Actually Rewards

Platforms do not reward volume; they reward engagement-per-post and account consistency. Five weak posts a week teach the algorithm your content gets ignored — actively harming your reach per post. Two strong posts teach it the opposite. Every account I manage that grew did so on quality-consistency, not quantity. The follower who sees one genuinely good post a week trusts you more than one who sees daily filler.

The Honest Numbers

For a typical small business: two to three feed posts a week is the sustainable sweet spot — enough presence to stay alive in feeds and credible to profile visitors, low enough to maintain quality indefinitely. Stories are the free multiplier: post them daily if you like, because they cost minutes, disappear in 24 hours, and keep you at the front of the story bar without cluttering your grid. Reels count within the two-three, not on top of it.

The Real Rule: Pick a Floor You Cannot Miss

Choose a frequency you can hit on your worst week — sick, travelling, festival season — and treat it as a floor, not a target. A business that posts twice a week for a year beats one that posts daily for a month, four times over. Consistency compounds; bursts evaporate. And when a week genuinely defeats you, one honest behind-the-scenes story beats silence.

The right posting frequency is the one that survives your busiest month. Everything above that floor is bonus. Everything promised beyond it is a future dead account.

WhatsApp Business — The Underrated Sales Tool for Indian SMEs

Social Media & Business · 2 July 2026 · 6 min read

Every Indian business uses WhatsApp. Almost none use WhatsApp Business correctly.

The Tool Everyone Has, Almost Nobody Uses Correctly

Nearly every small business in India already communicates with customers over WhatsApp. Almost none of them use WhatsApp Business — the free, dedicated business version of the app — correctly, or sometimes at all, instead running their entire customer communication through a personal WhatsApp account with no catalogue, no automated responses, and no professional presentation.

This is a missed opportunity that costs nothing to fix. WhatsApp Business is free, takes under an hour to set up properly, and consistently increases enquiry-to-sale conversion for the small businesses I have set it up for.

The Business Profile — First Impressions Matter Here Too

A WhatsApp Business profile includes a business name, category, description, address, website link, and business hours — all visible the moment someone opens a chat with you, before they have even sent a message. A complete, professional profile signals legitimacy immediately. An account with just a phone number and a personal-looking display photo signals the opposite, regardless of how good the actual product or service is.

Fill in every available field in the business profile completely. This takes ten minutes and is one of the highest-return, lowest-effort improvements available to almost any small business communicating with customers over WhatsApp.

The Catalogue — A Free Storefront

WhatsApp Business allows you to build a product or service catalogue directly within the app — photos, descriptions, and prices for everything you offer, browsable by any customer who messages you, without needing a separate website or e-commerce platform. For many small businesses, this catalogue functions as a complete, free, always-accessible product listing that customers can browse and reference before or during a conversation.

Build the catalogue with the same care you would put into a website product page — clear photos, accurate descriptions, current prices. A half-finished, outdated catalogue undermines trust the same way an outdated website does.

Quick Replies and Automated Messages

Quick replies let you save common responses — pricing information, availability, frequently asked questions — and insert them instantly rather than retyping the same answer repeatedly throughout the day. Automated greeting messages welcome new contacts immediately, even outside business hours. Automated away messages set expectations when you are unavailable, rather than leaving a customer wondering if they have been ignored.

These features take minutes to set up and meaningfully improve response time and customer experience, particularly for solo operators and small teams who cannot realistically respond to every message within minutes around the clock.

Labels — Organizing Without a CRM

WhatsApp Business includes a labelling system that lets you tag conversations — new enquiry, in progress, payment pending, completed — directly within the app. For a small business not yet ready to invest in a dedicated CRM system, this labelling feature provides a surprisingly functional, free alternative for tracking where each customer conversation stands, as long as labels are applied consistently.

Broadcast Lists for Repeat Communication

Broadcast lists allow sending a single message to multiple contacts simultaneously, with each recipient seeing it as an individual message rather than a group chat — useful for announcing new arrivals, promotions, or updates to a customer list who has previously opted in. This must be used carefully and only with contacts who have genuinely agreed to receive such messages, both to remain within WhatsApp's usage policies and to avoid customers blocking the business number for unwanted messages.

WhatsApp Business is not a minor feature upgrade. For most small businesses in India, it is the actual primary sales channel, disguised as a messaging app. Treating it with the same professionalism as a storefront or website, rather than as a casual chat, is one of the highest-return changes available, free, in under an hour of setup.

Setting It Up Properly This Week

Download WhatsApp Business if not already using it, and migrate your existing business number to it. Complete every field in the business profile. Build a basic catalogue, even a partial one to start. Set up a welcome message and an away message. Begin using labels consistently from this point forward. None of this costs money. All of it improves how your business is perceived from the very first message.

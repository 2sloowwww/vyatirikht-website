Policy

Refund &
Cancellation

Last updated: 19 July 2026

In short: contributions to the news bot are voluntary and generally non-refundable, but if you contributed by mistake, changed your mind within 7 days, or were charged twice, write to us and we will refund you. Paid studio work is governed by its own written agreement.

1. What this policy covers

Vyatirikht takes money in two quite different ways, and they are treated differently:

  • Voluntary contributions made through the Support page, which help cover the running costs of the automated news writing on our blog.
  • Creative studio services — photography, filmmaking, branding, and social media work — which are commissioned directly and invoiced separately.

2. Contributions to the news bot

Nature of the payment

A contribution is a voluntary payment in support of freely available work. It is not a purchase. Nothing on this site is placed behind a paywall, no goods are shipped, and contributing does not buy you a service, a subscription, editorial influence, or any form of ownership. Everything the bot publishes stays free to read whether you contribute or not.

Because there is no product or service being bought, contributions are, as a general rule, non-refundable.

When we will refund you anyway

We would rather return money than keep money someone did not mean to give. We will refund a contribution in full if you contact us within 7 days of the payment and any of the following applies:

  • You were charged more than once for the same intended contribution.
  • You entered the wrong amount — for example an extra zero.
  • The payment was made without the account holder's knowledge or permission.
  • You simply changed your mind.
  • A technical fault on our side took the money without recording the contribution.

You do not have to justify a change of mind. Asking within the window is enough.

Requests after 7 days

We will still look at them. Once funds have been spent on research and model costs we cannot always return them, but unauthorised or duplicate charges will be refunded regardless of how long it has been.

How to request a refund

Email 2sloowwww@gmail.com from the address you used when contributing, or use the form on our contact section. Please include the approximate date, the amount, and the Razorpay payment ID if you have it — it appears in the receipt email and begins with pay_.

We aim to acknowledge refund requests within 2 business days and to approve or decline them within 5 business days.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are issued through Razorpay to the original payment method only — the same card, UPI handle, or bank account. We cannot redirect a refund elsewhere. Once we initiate it, Razorpay and your bank typically take 5–10 business days to place the money back in your account. That part of the timeline is outside our control.

Refunds are made in Indian Rupees for the amount originally received. If your bank applied a currency conversion or levied its own charges, those are set by your bank and we cannot reimburse them.

Cancellation

Contributions are one-time payments. There is no recurring billing, no subscription, and therefore nothing to cancel. We do not store your card details — see our Privacy Policy. A contribution can be stopped at any point before you confirm payment on the Razorpay page simply by closing that window; nothing is charged until you confirm.

3. Studio services

Commissioned work — shoots, films, branding, and retainers — is not booked or paid for through this website. Each engagement is agreed in writing beforehand, and that agreement governs its own deposit, cancellation, rescheduling, and refund terms, including any non-refundable booking amount held against a reserved date.

Where no written agreement exists, we will deal with cancellations reasonably and in good faith: work not yet begun is refundable, and work already delivered is not. If a shoot is cancelled by us, or we cannot deliver what was agreed, you get a full refund of anything paid toward it.

4. Chargebacks

If something has gone wrong, please contact us before raising a dispute with your bank. A chargeback is slower for you than a direct refund and costs us a penalty fee on top of the disputed amount. We have never refused a genuine refund request and would rather resolve it directly.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site changes. The revision date at the top always reflects the current version, and any change applies only to payments made after it is published.

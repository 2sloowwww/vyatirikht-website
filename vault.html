Free After Effects templates, from a working studio

These are the After Effects templates I use on real client work at Vyatirikht, a creative studio in Pune — not filler built to be given away. They are free to download and free to use commercially, including in paid client projects.

Titles and lower thirds

Name plates and caption builds for interviews, brand films and case studies. Text, colour and timing are editable, and the builds use standard After Effects effects rather than third-party plugins, so nothing breaks when you open them on a different machine.

Logo stings and reveals

Short brand animations for the opening and closing of a film. Each has a placeholder composition — drop your logo in and the animation picks it up without rebuilding anything.

Reels and Stories templates

Vertical builds at 1080 × 1920, the 9:16 frame Instagram Reels, Stories and YouTube Shorts expect. Safe areas are marked, so captions and interface elements never sit on top of your text.

Transitions and overlays

Cuts, light leaks and grain passes chosen because they suit brand and documentary work, rather than the flashy ones that date a video within a year.

Common questions

Are these After Effects templates really free?

Yes. Free to download, free to use in client work. I ask for an email so I can tell you when new packs land. There is no payment step and no trial period.

Which version of After Effects do I need?

They open in Creative Cloud releases from roughly 2022 onward and use built-in effects only — no Element 3D, no Optical Flares, nothing you need to buy separately.

Can I use them in paid client projects?

Yes, commercially and without attribution. What you may not do is resell or redistribute the template files themselves, or upload them to a stock site as your own. Full terms are in my terms and conditions.

How do I edit the text?

Open the project, find the composition marked for editing, and change the text layers directly. Type, colour and timing are all adjustable.

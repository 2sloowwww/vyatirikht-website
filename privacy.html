Policy

Last updated: 19 July 2026

Short version: you can read this entire site without giving us anything. We only hold what you type into a form yourself. We never see your card details. We don't run advertising or tracking analytics, and we don't sell or share your information. Ask us and we will delete what we hold.

1. Scope

This policy explains what Vyatirikht (Pune, Maharashtra, India) does with personal information collected through this website. It covers the site only — it does not cover information exchanged during a commissioned engagement, which is handled under the agreement for that work.

2. Reading the site

Browsing the site and reading the blog requires no account and no personal information. We do not run advertising networks, behavioural tracking, or third-party analytics on this site, and we do not build profiles of readers.

3. What we collect, and only when you give it

Enquiry form

If you contact us through the form on the homepage, we store your name and phone number (both required), and your email address, the service you're interested in, and your message where you provide them. We use it solely to reply to you and to keep track of the conversation.

Newsletter

If you subscribe, we store your email address and a note of which page you subscribed from. It is used only to send occasional updates about new writing. Every email includes a way to unsubscribe, or you can email us and we will remove you immediately.

Contributions

If you contribute through the Support page we store the amount, the Razorpay order and payment identifiers, and the payment status — plus your name, email address, and message if you chose to enter them. All three of those are optional; you may contribute anonymously. We use this to confirm the payment, send a receipt where an email was given, keep proper financial records, and process refunds.

We never receive your payment details. Card numbers, UPI IDs, CVVs, and bank credentials are entered on Razorpay's own secure page and are never transmitted to, processed by, or stored on this website. We only ever see a confirmation that a payment succeeded.

Team accounts

Vyatirikht team members have accounts holding an email address and a securely hashed password. These are created by us; there is no public sign-up.

4. Why we are allowed to hold it

We process this information because you gave it to us voluntarily and asked us to do something with it (reply to an enquiry, send the newsletter, process a contribution); because we have a legitimate interest in running and securing the site; and because we are legally obliged to retain financial records.

5. Where it is stored

Site data is stored in a Supabase Postgres database with row-level security enabled, so that submissions are write-only from the public site and readable only by authenticated Vyatirikht team members. Payment records are also held by Razorpay as our payment processor. Both are third-party providers with their own security and privacy practices, and data may be stored on servers outside India.

6. Who else sees it

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and we do not share it for anyone else's marketing. It is seen only by:

  • Vyatirikht team members who need it to do their job.
  • Supabase, as our database and authentication host.
  • Razorpay, as our payment processor, for contributions.
  • Anyone we are legally required to disclose to under Indian law.

7. Third-party services on this site

  • Google Fonts — typefaces are loaded from Google's servers, which means your browser contacts Google and your IP address is visible to them as part of that request.
  • Razorpay Checkout — the payment script loads only on the Support page.
  • Supabase — receives form submissions and serves blog articles.

Our automated news writing sends topic prompts and public web search results to an AI provider to draft articles. Your personal information is never part of that process.

8. Cookies

This site sets no advertising or tracking cookies. Where you are signed in to the team area, your browser stores a session token so you stay logged in; that is functional, not tracking, and clearing your browser storage removes it.

9. How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept while the conversation is active and for a reasonable period afterwards. Newsletter addresses are kept until you unsubscribe. Contribution records are kept as long as tax and accounting law requires, typically eight years. Anything no longer needed is deleted.

10. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct anything that is wrong.
  • Delete what we hold, where we are not legally required to keep it.
  • Unsubscribe from the newsletter.
  • Withdraw consent you previously gave.

Email 2sloowwww@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. There is no charge. Note that financial records attached to a contribution must be retained for the statutory period even if you ask for deletion — we will delete the optional name, email, and message from that record instead.

11. Security

Submissions travel over HTTPS. The database enforces row-level security so that public visitors can submit information but cannot read anyone's submissions back. Passwords are hashed, never stored in readable form. Payment credentials never touch our systems. No system is perfectly secure, but we take this seriously, and if a breach ever affects your information we will tell you.

12. Children

This site is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has submitted personal information to us, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes

If this policy changes, the revision date at the top will change with it. Material changes will be noted on the site.

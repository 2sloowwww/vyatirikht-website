Support

Help keep the
news bot running.

Every article the bot writes costs real money — it searches the web, reads sources, and drafts the piece using a paid AI model. If the writing has been useful to you, you can chip in toward what it costs. Entirely optional, and everything stays free to read either way.

What your money does

This isn't a charity and I won't pretend otherwise — Vyatirikht is a working studio. What donations do is stop the research budget being the reason a piece doesn't get written.

  • ₹100Covers roughly five researched articles.
  • ₹500About a month of the daily news beat.
  • ₹1,000Lets the bot run deeper research on harder topics.

Every bot-written article is reviewed by a human before it publishes, and lists the sources it used. That review is the part that keeps it honest — donations just keep the lights on.

Make a contribution

Payment is handled entirely by Razorpay — card and UPI details are entered on their secure page and never touch this site. A contribution is a voluntary gift, not a purchase, and it buys no influence over what I publish. See my refund policy, terms, and privacy policy.

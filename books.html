Book One

Anatomy of a Motorcycle

16 Parts 150 Chapters Complete

A field manual for understanding the machine — not what a motorcycle looks like, but why every part of it is shaped the way it is. Sixteen parts, from first principles through engines, chassis, suspension, physics, riding, maintenance and diagnosis, to how the trade-offs get made.

A motorcycle is not a smaller, cheaper car. It is a fundamentally different kind of machine, because it requires the rider to be part of the control system, not just an operator of it.

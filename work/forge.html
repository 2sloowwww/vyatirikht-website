// features

Everything a preview should be.

Three properties, and Forge refuses to ship a preview that misses any of them.

01

Instant

Cold deploy in single-digit seconds. Warm rebuilds land before you've switched windows.

02

Isolated

Every branch gets its own environment, database branch and secrets. Nothing leaks sideways.

03

Ephemeral

Torn down when the PR merges. No orphaned infrastructure, no bill creep at the end of the quarter.

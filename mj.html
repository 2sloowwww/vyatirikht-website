About

Creator. Builder.
Rider. Founder.

Jitendra Kulkarni — known as MJ — is the founder and creative director of VYATIRIKHT, a Pune-based creative studio built at the intersection of photography, film, branding, and digital strategy.

He started with a camera and a compulsion to document the world differently. What followed was a discipline that spans product photography, automotive filmmaking, portrait work, maternity sessions, event coverage, and creative direction for brands — all shot and directed in-house.

Outside the studio, MJ rides motorcycles, writes about entrepreneurship, and builds things. Not as hobbies — as ways of thinking. The same precision that goes into a frame goes into every system, process, and decision at Vyatirikht.
